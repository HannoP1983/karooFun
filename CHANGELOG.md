# Changelog

**English** | [Deutsch](CHANGELOG.de.md)

## 1.5.1

- **Elevation · Climbed**: meters climbed in the ride, measured in landmarks from Cologne Cathedral to Everest
- **Elevation · Now**: altitude above sea level with a pin on the mountain, snow from 1500 m, from "sea breeze" to
  "above the Zugspitze!"
- **Elevation · Up & down**: meters climbed (+) and descended (−), the balance takes turns with the field name

## 1.5

**More fun in the fields**

- **Watts** as a household appliance: light bulbs, blender, toaster, hair dryer, kettle
- **Ø Watts**: the work done so far, converted into espressos
- **Power zone** with a character per zone, from coffee-ride sloth to rocket
- **% FTP** as a pot on the stove: lukewarm, simmering, bubbling, boiling over
- **Gear**: "Coffee grinder!" and "Tractor!" depending on cadence, "KRRRK!" when cross-chained, and a question after 20 minutes in the same gear
- **Calories** in pretzels, bratwursts, pizza, döner and wheat beer, taking turns
- **Grade**: sweat from 6 %, "Walking section?" from 10 %, "THE WALL!" from 15 %, "Wheeee!" downhill
- **To summit**: a climber moves up the mountain; on top summit cross, confetti and summit beer
- **Next turn**: "NOW! Don't miss it!", and off the route "Recalculating…" with the way back
- **Rain from**: jacket countdown; rubber ducks while it rains
- **Wind**: free watts with tailwind, blow-dried hair from 30 km/h headwind
- **Sunset in** with a photo stop in the last half hour, **Lights on** with bats at night
- Remarks take turns with the field name, so the big number stays free

**Three new comic actions**, each with its own settings card and preview

- **🏔 Summit!** when you top out a climb on your route (from 50 m of climbing)
- **⚡ Kilowatt club** when your 3 s power breaks 1000 W
- **🍲 Boiling over** after 2 minutes above 105 % FTP

**Rain radar**

- Forecast in 5-minute steps, reloaded every 10 minutes
- Smooth, round rain areas instead of 1 km squares
- More precise outlook: the surroundings of about 1 km instead of a single pixel, and each forecast is checked
  where you will be by then; "no data" at the edge of the radar no longer counts as rain
- Radar and weather wait for a real GPS fix; failed requests are retried sooner
- The map is only loaded once the radar tile is used
